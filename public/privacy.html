Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Travel Planner is a free, personal trip-planning app. This page explains what information the app handles and what happens to it.

Using the app without signing in

Your trips, itineraries, expenses and notes are stored only in your own browser on your own device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.

Signing in with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in so you can share a trip, the app uses Google Sign-In to learn your email address and name. We use these only to identify you, to show other people on a shared trip who has access, and to keep your account signed in. We do not request access to your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data.

Shared trips

When you turn on sharing for a trip, a copy of that trip (its itinerary, flights, hotels, expenses and who paid for what) is stored online with Supabase, our database provider. It is visible and editable only to the people who have joined the trip using an invite link created by the trip's owner. Your email address is shown to the other members of the trips you join.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not show ads or use analytics or tracking cookies.
  • We do not share your information with anyone except the other members of a trip you chose to share.

Your choices

  • You can stop sharing a trip, remove people from it, or leave a shared trip at any time inside the app.
  • Deleting a trip you own removes the shared copy for everyone.
  • To have your account and any shared trips you own removed, contact us using the link below.

Contact
